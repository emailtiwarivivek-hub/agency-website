## Write for the deal, not the search volume

The highest-value pages we have built answer objections that come up in week three of a sales cycle. They get four hundred visits a month and influence a third of closed revenue.

## Mine the calls

Recorded sales calls are the best content brief you will ever get. Transcribe a quarter of them and the topics write themselves.

## Measure influence, not attribution

Content rarely gets last click. Look at whether opportunities that touched a page close at a different rate.
