## The audit

We logged views on every dashboard across four accounts for sixty days. Eleven of fifty-one had been opened more than twice. Most of those were opened by the person who built them.

## Build for a decision

Every view should answer a question someone asks on a recurring cadence and can act on. If nobody can name the decision, delete the chart.

## What replaced them

One operator view per account, one weekly digest, one quarterly model. That is it.
