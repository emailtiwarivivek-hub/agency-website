## Frequency is a lagging signal

By the time frequency looks bad, CPA has already moved. Watch first-time-impression conversion rate instead — it turns days earlier.

## Rotate on a schedule, not a symptom

If you have the volume, retire assets on a fixed cadence regardless of performance. It is cheaper than the detection lag.

## Keep a bench

Three tested concepts waiting is the difference between a rotation and a scramble.
