## Diagnose the constraint first

If you do not know whether the problem is positioning, creative or measurement, you cannot know who to hire. A six-week diagnostic is cheaper than a wrong twelve-month hire.

## The generalist window

Below roughly $10M, one strong generalist plus contractors beats three specialists almost every time.

## Write the scorecard

Three outcomes, measurable, twelve-month horizon. If you cannot write them, you are not ready to hire.
