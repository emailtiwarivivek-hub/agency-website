## The incrementality gap

Platform ROAS answers a question nobody asked: of the people who saw this ad and converted, how much did they spend? The question you need answered is whether they would have converted without it.

## Run the holdout

A geo holdout is the cheapest honest answer available. Split comparable markets, suppress the channel in half, run four weeks, compare. It costs you a little revenue and buys you a number you can take to a board.

In the last eleven holdouts we have run, the median incrementality of branded search was 34%. The mean of what the platform reported was 100%.

## What changes

Freed budget almost never goes back into the same channel. It goes to prospecting, or to lifecycle, or to a creative programme nobody would fund while the dashboard looked healthy.
