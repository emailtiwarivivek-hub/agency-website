## The incentive

If our revenue rises with your budget, the recommendation to spend less costs us money every month it is followed. No amount of integrity survives that indefinitely.

## What we do instead

Flat fees, published, reviewed annually. When we tell a client to cut spend by 30% — which we have done three times this year — it costs us nothing.

## The counter-argument

Percentage fees scale effort with complexity, which is fair. Tiered flat fees do the same thing without the conflict.
