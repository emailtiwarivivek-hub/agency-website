## We over-indexed on incrementality

Holdouts are excellent and we ran too many of them, on channels too small to produce a readable result. Three of last year's tests were unpowered and we reported them anyway.

## We took a client we should have declined

The constraint was product, we knew it in week two, and we spent seven months proving it with marketing spend instead of saying so.

## We under-invested in the studio

Demand for creative volume outran capacity for most of the year. Two hires late.

## We wrote too little

This blog went quiet for five months while we were busy, which is exactly when the writing is most useful.
