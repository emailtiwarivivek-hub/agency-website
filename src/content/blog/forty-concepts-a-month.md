## Modularity over volume

Ten shoots do not produce ten assets. Structured properly they produce a library of hooks, proof segments and closes that recombine into forty distinct concepts, each testing one variable.

## Every asset is a hypothesis

If you cannot write the sentence 'we believe X audience responds to Y because Z' the asset does not get made. This kills roughly a third of ideas before production, which is the point.

## Kill things fast

A concept gets a fixed budget and a fixed window. At the end it scales or it is buried. Nobody defends their own work in the read-out, because the work is not theirs — it belongs to the system.
