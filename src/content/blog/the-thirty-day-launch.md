## Parallel, not sequential

The diagnostic and the build run at the same time. You do not need a finished positioning platform to start testing hooks, and the tests make the platform better.

## What day thirty looks like

Tracking reconciled, three creative territories in market, one channel restructured and a forecast you can argue with. Not a strategy document.

## The trade-off

You will make some decisions on incomplete information. That is fine, because you will be reading real data four weeks earlier than the alternative.
