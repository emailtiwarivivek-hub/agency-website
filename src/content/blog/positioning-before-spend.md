## Undifferentiated creative costs more

When five competitors make the same claim, the auction prices you all identically and the click goes to whoever bid most. Differentiation is the only lever that changes the cost of attention rather than the amount of it.

## Find the defensible thing

Not the nicest thing you can say. The thing that is true, matters to a buyer, and that a competitor would struggle to copy inside a year.

## Then let media prove it

Positioning claims are hypotheses too. Put three of them into paid social for two weeks before you put any of them on the homepage.
